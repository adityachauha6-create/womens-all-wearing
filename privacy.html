Privacy Policy

Womens all wearing

Effective date: October 6, 2026

1. Overview

Womens all wearing is a personal media automation application operated by its owner. This policy explains how information accessed through the Pinterest API is used by the application.

2. Pinterest Data

The application may access Pins and boards belonging to the Pinterest account that authorizes the application, using permissions granted through Pinterest OAuth.

3. How Data Is Used

Authorized Pinterest data is used to organize and process media for the owner's personal automation workflow. Selected media may be delivered to a private Telegram group controlled by the owner.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell Pinterest data or use it for advertising. Pinterest account data is not intentionally shared with unrelated third parties. Data may be transmitted to services required to perform the requested automation, such as Telegram.

5. Security

Authentication credentials and access tokens should be kept confidential and are not intended to be publicly disclosed.

6. Revoking Access

The account owner can revoke the application's Pinterest authorization through Pinterest account settings or by discontinuing use of the application.

7. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data practices change.
